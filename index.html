How to Use the Media Mister SMM Panel

  1. Register with your email address or Google account. Follow the verification steps and complete your profile to access the panel.

  2. Open Wallet, choose the amount to add, and select an available payment option. Review the funding bonus, payment total, and any applicable tax before completing your deposit.

  3. Open New Order and choose your platform, followed by the service. Select the available type, audience, and quantity options that match your requirements.

  4. Provide the requested username, profile URL, content link, or service-specific information. Check the instructions carefully, including any public-account requirements.

  5. Check the order summary, price, and delivery estimate. Pay using your available wallet balance, then follow progress from the Orders section.

Reward points are separate from the amount immediately added to your wallet.

Redeem Points for Future Orders

Every 100 points converts to $1 in wallet credit. Redemption starts at 100 points, and you can redeem amounts up to your available points balance. For example, 125 points gives you $1.25 in wallet credit.

Points and membership levels never expire. Spending your wallet balance does not reduce your membership level.

Use the rewards calculator to preview your benefits before adding funds. When a deposit crosses a membership threshold, the higher rate applies only to the portion above that threshold. Redeemed points become wallet credit for orders.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Media Mister SMM Panel

Answers about the panel, wallet, rewards, delivery, API and MCP.

01What Is an SMM Panel?

An SMM panel, short for social media marketing panel, is an online dashboard for purchasing and managing social media services such as followers, likes, views, and comments. The Media Mister Panel combines these purchases with wallet payments, order tracking, rewards, and support.

02How Is the Panel Different From Ordering on the Media Mister Website?

The panel provides an account-based workflow where you add wallet funds, place orders, and manage purchases from a dashboard. It also includes membership rewards, subscription controls, invoices, developer tools, and linked support tickets.

03Which Social Media Platforms Are Supported?

The catalog includes services for TikTok, YouTube, Spotify, X, Telegram, Twitch, and many other platforms. Open Services to see the current platform list, available service types, quantities, and prices.

04Can I Choose a Target Country?

Yes, country targeting is available for supported services. Choose your platform and service first, then review its audience options. Available countries depend on the service and variant you select.

05What Is the Minimum Order Quantity?

The minimum quantity depends on the selected service. Check its package options or quantity limits before ordering. For example, the Standard Worldwide TikTok Followers option includes a 25-follower package.

06How Do I Pay for Orders?

Panel orders are paid from your wallet balance. Open Wallet to add funds using an available payment option, then use your balance when placing an order. Review the payment total and any applicable tax before completing a deposit.

07Do I Need to Share My Social Media Password?

No. Media Mister does not require your social media password to deliver an order. Enter the username, profile link, content URL, or other information requested by the service form.

08Does My Profile Need to Be Public?

Follow the visibility requirements shown for your selected service. For example, TikTok follower orders require a public account, and the form instructs you to keep your username unchanged after ordering.

09How Long Does Delivery Take?

Delivery estimates depend on the service, quantity, and selected options. Review the timeframe in your order summary before paying, then check Orders for progress. Delivery start time and full completion time are separate estimates.

10What Is the Difference Between Bonus Credit and Reward Points?

Bonus credit is added to your wallet immediately with a qualifying deposit. Reward points are earned separately and can be redeemed for additional wallet credit once you have at least 100 points.

11Do My Rewards or Membership Level Expire?

No. Reward points and membership levels do not expire. Your level is based on lifetime funding, so spending your wallet balance does not lower it.

12Can I Withdraw Reward Points as Cash?

Reward points are redeemed as USD wallet credit for Media Mister orders. Every 100 points gives you $1 in wallet credit; redemption does not create a cash payout.

13Can I Manage Subscriptions and Download Invoices?

Yes. Use Subscriptions to review and manage supported plans. Order and wallet-top-up invoices are available once your billing details are complete.

14What Is the Difference Between the API and MCP?

The API connects your software to Media Mister through code. MCP connects compatible AI assistants to five supported functions: checking balances, browsing services, placing orders, and checking individual or multiple order statuses. Both use the same backend and wallet.

15Can My Existing Media Mister Account Access MCP?

Yes. You can use your existing Media Mister account to access MCP. Generate your API key in Settings, connect a compatible AI assistant, and add sufficient wallet funds before placing orders.

16How Do I Contact Support?

Open a support ticket in the panel and link the relevant order or wallet transaction. Add your question and any useful attachments. You can also contact Media Mister through live chat or email at info@mediamister.com.
